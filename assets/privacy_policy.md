## Data Collected

### AniList OAuth (Optional)
If you choose to log in with AniList, the app collects:
- Your AniList username and avatar URL
- Your AniList user ID
- This data is used solely to display your AniList profile and sync your anime lists

### Watch History and Preferences
- Episode titles and progress timestamps
- Favorite anime list
- App preferences (theme, language, source selection)
- All stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences

### No Automatic Data Collection
- No analytics SDKs are included
- No crash reporting SDKs are included
- No advertising SDKs are included
- No personal information is collected by the app server-side

## Data Sources

The app fetches publicly available anime metadata from:
- AnimeFire (animefire.net)
- AllAnime (allanime.to)
- SuperFlix (superflix.pro)
- Goyabu (goyabu.com)

These sources are scraped client-side. No user data is sent to these services.

## Data Sharing

No user data is shared with any third party.

## Data Retention

Watch history and preferences are stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. AniList OAuth token is stored locally. You can clear all app data via Android system settings.

## Third-Party Services

- **AniList API (GraphQL)** — Used for catalog enrichment and user lists. Subject to AniList's own privacy policy.
- **GitHub** — Source code repository. No user data transmitted.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
